# Teardown — Kitchen Hole vs the best

Competitor analysis source: `comunicacion-ias/analisis-competencia/
analisis-competencia-youtube-playables-2026-11-15.md` (issue #22).
Rule: beat the best in ≥3 points. This game is measured against the two
closest top Playables plus the genre reference.

## References

| Game | Plays | What it does best |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge Race | 71M | Eat the map with decisions — collecting IS the game; one clear verb |
| Snake Clash! | 107M | Growing IS the game — size is visible progress, no HUD needed |
| hole.io (genre ref) | — | The hole fantasy: swallow a city; scale escalation as the joke |

## Where Kitchen Hole beats them

1. **Comic scale escalation as the joke (vs hole.io).** hole.io grows
   you from bench to building with no story. Here the ladder IS the
   fiction: crumb → croissant → chair → table → **fridge with a face**.
   Every kitchen ends on a punchline object the player can name. The
   fridge smiles while you eat it.
2. **Growth you can see without the HUD (vs Bridge Race).** Bridge Race
   needs its progress bar; Snake Clash! proves size can BE the progress.
   Here the hole's radius is the progress — the SIZE bar only mirrors
   it. A spectator understands the whole game in 3 seconds of watching.
3. **A kitchen with a story per level (vs both).** Bridge Race and Snake
   Clash! repeat one arena. Here each block is a place (Grandma's wood
   kitchen, the food truck, the bakery, the pizzeria, the sushi bar, the
   grand kitchen) with its own floor, palette and toy: the dog that
   steals coins (L6), golden food (L11), the fleeing mouse (L16), the
   fridge (L21). A new toy per block keeps the first 30 levels fresh.
4. **Risk/reward the references lack.** The pet steals coins on touch
   (score threat, never a win threat — a fairer version of Snake's
   elimination), and golden food spawns near the pet's patrol: ×3 coins
   if you dare to eat next to the thief, gone in 8 s if you hesitate.
5. **A living menu (vs static menus everywhere).** The menu IS the game:
   the hole swallows a croissant on loop, the fridge smiles, tap it and
   it laughs. Zero reading before the first tap; the tutorial is a hand
   following the planned route on level 1.

## Where it does NOT beat them (honest)

- **No multiplayer / no live opponents.** Snake Clash! and hole.io get
  tension from other snakes/holes. Here the tension is the timer + the
  pet only. Single-player by design (Playgama Playable scope); a
  "rival hole" mode is a round-2 idea, not a round-1 promise.
- **No meta-progression beyond skins + extra time.** Bridge Race has
  unlockable skins tied to wins; we have 5 hole skins + extra-time
  refills. The collection hook (kitchens + holes) is sketched in the
  concept ("collection of kitchens and holes") but only the 6 kitchens
  ship in round 1.
- **Precise-policy clear times are fast (1.4–5.0 s).** A perfect player
  clears any level in seconds against a 60–74 s timer. The challenge
  for humans is routing + pet + goldens, not survival — the difficulty
  verdict is NOT YET MEASURED until the 3-tester human playtest.

## Verdict

Beats the references on 5 points (scale joke, visible growth, story per
kitchen, risk/reward, living menu), concedes multiplayer and deep meta.
The hook in 10 s is the first swallow + the hole visibly growing — the
same hook the concept promised, delivered by the fit rule + growth
ladder, verified by the harness (solution 30/30, idle 0/30).
