# Short script — Kitchen Hole (YouTube Shorts, 9:16, 1080×1920)

Format: 15–35 s, opens on GAMEPLAY in the first frame, ≤4 huge words in
the first 2 s. Only real gameplay on screen — never footage of another
game. Filming: capture from dist-test in Chromium (finisher window) —
script + storyboard delivered now, video file pending the capture.

## Hooks (hook-writer: 10 candidates, scored)

| # | Hook (on screen, first 2 s) | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "EAT THE FRIDGE" | 9/10 | **RECOMMENDED.** The punchline object, the whole joke in 3 words |
| 2 | "FROM CRUMB TO FRIDGE" | 8/10 | The scale ladder in 4 words |
| 3 | "ONE HOLE. ONE KITCHEN." | 7/10 | Clean, but no payoff |
| 4 | "THE PET STOLE MY COINS" | 7/10 | Character + conflict |
| 5 | "HOW BIG CAN IT GET?" | 7/10 | Curiosity, weak visual |
| 6 | "SWALLOW EVERYTHING" | 6/10 | Accurate, generic |
| 7 | "GOLDEN FOOD = x3" | 6/10 | Mechanic, not emotion |
| 8 | "30 KITCHENS. 1 HOLE." | 6/10 | Scope, no hook moment |
| 9 | "DON'T LET THE CAT WIN" | 6/10 | Fun, niche |
| 10 | "CLEAN THE KITCHEN… BY EATING IT" | 8/10 | Runner-up: the win condition as a joke |

**Selected: #1 "EAT THE FRIDGE"** — it is the game's final image
(L21/L30), the strongest visual, and the most shareable line.
