Long description (EN):
Ahoy, treasure hunter! On a sunny pirate beach, chests burst out of the sand — tap them before they sink back down. Wood is worth 10, silver 25, gold 50... but gold sinks fast. Watch out: bombs cost a life, and a cheeky crab will steal your chests if you let it reach them. And somewhere among hundreds of chests hides the legendary GOLDEN DOUBLOON — the odds are right there on screen: 1 in 500. Can YOU find it?

- One-touch arcade: tap, tap, tap — nothing else to learn
- 45-60 second rounds, perfect for a quick break
- Combos up to x3, stars on every level, 30 levels on a treasure map
- Daily challenge with better doubloon odds and a streak to keep
- No ads during play, no real money, no purchases — just points and glory
